Read the whole Bible

Choose your pace. We keep track.

Pick your Bible: King James, the Berean Standard Bible in modern English, or the Catholic Douay-Rheims with all 73 books. It’s all built into the app. Choose 3 months, 6 months, 1 year or 2 years, and PrayerChair gives you a few chapters each day and shows whether you’re on track.

  • 3 months: about 15 chapters a day
  • 1 year: about 4 chapters a day
  • Search by reference or by word
  • Switch Bibles anytime
Bible word games

Four games that make Scripture stick

  • Crossword: every clue is a verse with one word missing
  • Verse Builder: tap the words back into order
  • Cryptogram: crack the coded verse
  • Word Search: find the verse’s key words
  • 2 players: text a friend a code and race on the same crossword
