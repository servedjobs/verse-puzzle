Verse Puzzle Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026 · Verse Puzzle is a Shortround Gaming title, published by Vandelay Labs, a trade name of Vandelay Trucking LLC (“we”).

Verse Puzzle is a Bible verse puzzle game. It is built to work without collecting anything about you. This policy explains what that means.

What we collect

Nothing. Verse Puzzle does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools in the app.

What stays on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves a small amount of information on your own phone: your puzzle levels, daily streak, hints, and settings such as sound, vibration, text size and the daily reminder. This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the app or clearing its storage removes it. If your phone’s own backup service is turned on, your phone may include this data in your personal device backup; that backup is handled by your device maker or Google under their own terms, not by us.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks your phone for permission to show notifications and schedules a local reminder on the device. No server is involved, and you can turn it off in the app’s settings or your phone’s settings at any time.

Sharing a verse

When you tap “Share verse,” the app opens your phone’s standard share menu with the verse text. You choose where it goes. We do not receive a copy.

Internet use

All verses and puzzles are built into the app. The app does not need an internet connection and does not send data to us or to anyone else.

Children

Verse Puzzle does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Scripture

Scripture quotations are from the King James Version, which is in the public domain.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If the app ever begins to collect any data, we will say so here before that version is released.
