PrayerChair

Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026 · PrayerChair is published by Shortround Studio (Vandelay Trucking LLC), “we” below.

PrayerChair collects nothing about you. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. PrayerChair does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information or usage data. The app works without an internet connection; the whole Bible, the explanations and the games are built in.

What stays on your phone

To remember your place, the app saves a small amount of information on your own device: your first name if you give one, the names of people you pray for, your reminder time, your Bible reading plan, your streak, saved verses, chapters read and game progress. It is used only to personalize the app on your phone. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the app removes it. If your phone’s own backup is on, it may be included in your personal backup, which Apple or Google handles under their own terms.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the app schedules your daily reading reminder and optional short reminders through the day on your phone itself. No server is involved. You can turn them off in the app’s settings or your phone’s settings.

Sharing a verse

When you tap Share, your phone’s standard share menu opens with the verse text. You choose where it goes. We do not receive a copy.

Children

PrayerChair does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Scripture

Scripture is from the Douay-Rheims Bible, Challoner revision, which is in the public domain.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If the app ever begins to collect data, we will say so here before that version is released.
